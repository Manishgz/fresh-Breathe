Questions, answered

What does AQI mean?

AQI turns pollutant levels into one number. On the India CPCB scale, 0–100 is good to satisfactory, 101–200 moderate, 201–300 poor, 301–400 very poor and 401+ severe.

Is the data on this page live?

No. All readings here are labelled demo samples. For live data, use official sources such as CPCB.

Which mask should I wear?

A well-fitted N95 or FFP2 mask helps outdoors on poor-air days. Cloth masks and dust scarves filter little fine PM2.5.

Who can see my pollution report?

Reports are public and unverified. Please don’t include personal details. For severe cases also contact your local pollution control board.

How can I reduce pollution at home?

Avoid burning waste, choose clean cooking fuel, save electricity, use public transport and plant trees.

Is this medical advice?

No. If you have breathing trouble, see a doctor and follow local public-health guidance.

Limitations of this website

Please read before relying on anything here.

📊

Demo data only

AQI, pollutant values, charts and map markers are generated samples. They are not live or official readings. For real data, use CPCB or your state pollution control board.

🩺

Not medical advice

Health tips are general awareness. If you have breathing trouble or a medical condition, see a doctor and follow local public-health guidance.

🤖

AI assistant can be wrong

Manish may make mistakes, is limited to air-pollution topics, and falls back to a few prepared answers when AI mode isn’t available.

📣

Reports are public and unverified

Pollution reports are not checked by any authority and are not forwarded to one. For severe cases, contact your local pollution control board or municipal helpline.

🗺️

Map is schematic

Station markers are approximate city positions, not exact monitoring sites. Only a few eastern and northern Indian cities are included. The 3D graph uses the same kind of demo values for 17 cities.

🧮

Calculator is an estimate

The eco-impact result uses average emission factors and rough assumptions, not a precise measurement of your footprint.

💾

Storage and backend

Data is kept in the page’s shared database when available, otherwise only in your own browser. There is no Node/Express + SQLite server, no alerts and no user accounts.

🌐

Browser and network needs

Voice input and output, the 3D globe and Google Maps depend on your browser, permissions and internet connection, and may not work everywhere.

🎓

Student project

AirGuard 3D is a school and hackathon project, not an official government or scientific service, and carries no guarantee of accuracy or availability.

About AirGuard 3D

AirGuard 3D is a school and hackathon project that explains air pollution, shows how air quality is read, and helps people act. Demo data is always labelled. Reports and subscriptions are stored in the page’s built-in shared database. The Node/Express + SQLite version from the project brief is not part of this hosted page.
